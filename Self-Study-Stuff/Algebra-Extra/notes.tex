\begin{prob*}
    Let 
    \[
        R = \frac{k[x,y]}{(xy)}
    \]
    and write $\overline x, \overline y$ for the residue classes. 

    What is $(\overline x)$ as an ideal of $R$? 

    Compute 
    \[
        R/(\bar x),\qquad R/(\bar y),\qquad R/(\bar x,\bar y).
    \]
    Then classify them as prime/maximal/neither. 
\end{prob*}
\begin{proof}[Sol]
    We have $\overline x = x+(xy)$. 
    
    The ideal generated by $\overline x$ is  
    \[
        (\overline x) = \{\overline{xf} \bigm| f \in k[x,y]\}. 
    \]

    Since $(xy) \subseteq (x)$ in $k[x,y]$ we 
    have 
    \[
        \frac{R}{(\overline x)} 
        = \frac{k[x,y]/(xy)}{(x)/(xy)}. 
    \]
    By the third isomorphism theorem, 
    \[
        \frac{R}{(\overline x)} \cong \frac{k[x,y]}{(x)} \cong k[y]. 
    \]

    Similarly, 
    \[
        \frac{R}{(\overline y)} \cong \frac{k[x,y]}{(y)} \cong k[x]. 
    \]

    The ideal generated by $(\overline x, \overline y)$ is 
    \[
        (\overline x, \overline y) 
        = \{\overline{xf} + \overline{yg} \bigm| 
        f,g \in k[x,y]\}. 
    \]

    Suppose $h \in (xy)$. 

    Then 
    \[
        h = \eta xy, \ \eta \in k[x,y].  
    \]

    Notice that 
    \[
        h = \eta xy = \eta \cdot x \cdot y + 0 \cdot y \in (x,y). 
    \]
    So as ideals of $k[x,y]$, 
    \[
        (xy) \subseteq (x,y). 
    \]

    Then 
    \[
        \frac{(x,y)}{(xy)} 
    \]
    is an ideal of $R = k[x,y]/(xy)$. 
    and 
    \[
        \frac{R}{(\overline x,\overline y)}
        \cong \frac{k[x,y]/(xy)}{(x,y)/(xy)} 
        \cong \frac{k[x,y]}{(x,y)} \cong k. 
    \]

    Since $k$ is a field we have that 
    $(\overline x, \overline y)$ is maximal. 

    For the other two we note that polynomial rings 
    over a field $k$ are integral domains, so 
    both $(\overline x), (\overline y)$ are prime, but not maximal 
    since $k[x]$ and $k[y]$ are not fields. 


\end{proof}
